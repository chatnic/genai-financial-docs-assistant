## Business Overview

Summit Ledger Software (SMLG) is a core banking software provider headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Marcus Chen and Chief Financial Officer Olivia Lindqvist. As of the end of fiscal 2024, Summit Ledger Software had 2,194 employees and served approximately 723 thousand customers. The company reports results in 2 segments: Subscription, Professional Services.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2024, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Subscription**: revenue of $301.8 million, or 45% of the total.
- **Professional Services**: revenue of $376.1 million, or 55% of the total.

Professional Services remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2024.

**Credit quality.** Deterioration in consumer or commercial credit quality could increase charge-offs and require higher loss reserves.

**Third-party vendors.** We depend on third-party technology vendors, and an outage at a key provider could interrupt service.

**Competition.** Competition from fintech companies and large banks could pressure pricing and customer acquisition.

**Catastrophe losses.** Severe weather and natural catastrophes could produce claims losses well above our expectations.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $677.9 million, which increased 8.5% from $624.5 million in fiscal 2023. Operating margin was 31.2%. Net income was $160.7 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Professional Services segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2024, Summit Ledger Software held cash and cash equivalents of $202.3 million and total debt of $233.6 million. During the year the company repurchased $4.2 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2025, management expects total revenue growth of 5% to 9%, driven by continued investment in Subscription and Professional Services.
