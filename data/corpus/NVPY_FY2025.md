## Business Overview

NovaPay Technologies (NVPY) is a payments processor headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Daniel Lindqvist and Chief Financial Officer Hannah Kowalski. As of the end of fiscal 2025, NovaPay Technologies had 6,462 employees and served approximately 1,161 thousand customers. The company reports results in 3 segments: Merchant Services, Issuer Processing, Cross-Border Payments.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2025, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Merchant Services**: revenue of $446.8 million, or 23% of the total.
- **Issuer Processing**: revenue of $716.0 million, or 37% of the total.
- **Cross-Border Payments**: revenue of $788.7 million, or 40% of the total.

Cross-Border Payments remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2025.

**Third-party vendors.** We depend on third-party technology vendors, and an outage at a key provider could interrupt service.

**Market volatility.** Declines in financial markets would reduce assets under management and the fees we earn on them.

**Cybersecurity.** A cybersecurity incident could disrupt our systems, expose customer data and result in regulatory penalties.

**Talent.** Our growth depends on attracting and retaining engineering and risk management talent.

<!-- section: management_discussion -->
## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $1.95 billion, which increased 5.4% from $1.85 billion in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 25.7%. Net income was $380.6 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Cross-Border Payments segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2025, NovaPay Technologies held cash and cash equivalents of $481.2 million and total debt of $1.19 billion. During the year the company repurchased $99.9 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2026, management expects total revenue growth of 2% to 4%, driven by continued investment in Merchant Services and Issuer Processing.
