## Business Overview

Harborline Financial (HBLF) is a regional bank headquartered in Portland, Maine. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Marcus Mehta and Chief Financial Officer Wei Chen. As of the end of fiscal 2024, Harborline Financial had 6,400 employees and served approximately 3,884 thousand customers. The company reports results in 3 segments: Consumer Banking, Commercial Banking, Wealth Management.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2024, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Consumer Banking**: revenue of $949.9 million, or 39% of the total.
- **Commercial Banking**: revenue of $1.11 billion, or 45% of the total.
- **Wealth Management**: revenue of $399.2 million, or 16% of the total.

Commercial Banking remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2024.

**Interest rates.** Changes in interest rates could compress our net interest margin and reduce demand for loans.

**Catastrophe losses.** Severe weather and natural catastrophes could produce claims losses well above our expectations.

**Talent.** Our growth depends on attracting and retaining engineering and risk management talent.

**Credit quality.** Deterioration in consumer or commercial credit quality could increase charge-offs and require higher loss reserves.

<!-- section: management_discussion -->
## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $2.46 billion, which increased 5.0% from $2.34 billion in fiscal 2023. Operating margin was 30.4%. Net income was $567.6 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Commercial Banking segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2024, Harborline Financial held cash and cash equivalents of $1.29 billion and total debt of $2.05 billion. During the year the company repurchased $111.9 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2025, management expects total revenue growth of 3% to 7%, driven by continued investment in Consumer Banking and Commercial Banking.
