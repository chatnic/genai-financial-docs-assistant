## Business Overview

Crestview Insurance Group (CRVI) is a property and casualty insurer headquartered in Hartford, Connecticut. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Robert Lindqvist and Chief Financial Officer Tomás Kowalski. As of the end of fiscal 2024, Crestview Insurance Group had 8,233 employees and served approximately 1,479 thousand customers. The company reports results in 2 segments: Personal Lines, Commercial Lines.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2024, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Personal Lines**: revenue of $1.66 billion, or 56% of the total.
- **Commercial Lines**: revenue of $1.31 billion, or 44% of the total.

Personal Lines remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2024.

**Competition.** Competition from fintech companies and large banks could pressure pricing and customer acquisition.

**Interest rates.** Changes in interest rates could compress our net interest margin and reduce demand for insurance products.

**Regulation.** New regulations, including changes to capital and consumer protection rules, could increase compliance costs.

**Market volatility.** Declines in financial markets would reduce assets under management and the fees we earn on them.

<!-- section: management_discussion -->
## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $2.98 billion, which decreased 2.2% from $3.04 billion in fiscal 2023. Operating margin was 11.8%. Net income was $265.8 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Personal Lines segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2024, Crestview Insurance Group held cash and cash equivalents of $1.04 billion and total debt of $1.73 billion. During the year the company repurchased $53.8 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2025, management expects total revenue growth of 5% to 8%, driven by continued investment in Personal Lines and Commercial Lines.
