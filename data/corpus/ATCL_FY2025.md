## Business Overview

Atlas Consumer Lending (ATCL) is a consumer lender headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Hannah Chen and Chief Financial Officer Samuel Lindqvist. As of the end of fiscal 2025, Atlas Consumer Lending had 3,503 employees and served approximately 3,579 thousand customers. The company reports results in 3 segments: Personal Loans, Auto Finance, Credit Cards.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2025, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Personal Loans**: revenue of $709.5 million, or 49% of the total.
- **Auto Finance**: revenue of $416.0 million, or 29% of the total.
- **Credit Cards**: revenue of $314.4 million, or 22% of the total.

Personal Loans remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2025.

**Third-party vendors.** We depend on third-party technology vendors, and an outage at a key provider could interrupt service.

**Credit quality.** Deterioration in consumer or commercial credit quality could increase charge-offs and require higher loss reserves.

**Talent.** Our growth depends on attracting and retaining engineering and risk management talent.

**Market volatility.** Declines in financial markets would reduce assets under management and the fees we earn on them.

<!-- section: management_discussion -->
## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $1.44 billion, which increased 12.2% from $1.28 billion in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 22.2%. Net income was $242.8 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Personal Loans segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2025, Atlas Consumer Lending held cash and cash equivalents of $497.9 million and total debt of $1.72 billion. During the year the company repurchased $77.0 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2026, management expects total revenue growth of 5% to 9%, driven by continued investment in Personal Loans and Auto Finance.
