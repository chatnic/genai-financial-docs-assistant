## Business Overview

Crestview Insurance Group (CRVI) is a property and casualty insurer headquartered in Hartford, Connecticut. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Robert Lindqvist and Chief Financial Officer Tomás Kowalski. As of the end of fiscal 2025, Crestview Insurance Group had 8,861 employees and served approximately 3,615 thousand customers. The company reports results in 2 segments: Personal Lines, Commercial Lines.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2025, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Personal Lines**: revenue of $1.41 billion, or 49% of the total.
- **Commercial Lines**: revenue of $1.50 billion, or 51% of the total.

Commercial Lines remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2025.

**Interest rates.** Changes in interest rates could compress our net interest margin and reduce demand for insurance products.

**Regulation.** New regulations, including changes to capital and consumer protection rules, could increase compliance costs.

**Market volatility.** Declines in financial markets would reduce assets under management and the fees we earn on them.

**Cybersecurity.** A cybersecurity incident could disrupt our systems, expose customer data and result in regulatory penalties.

<!-- section: management_discussion -->
## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $2.91 billion, which decreased 2.2% from $2.98 billion in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 7.8%. Net income was $172.1 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Commercial Lines segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2025, Crestview Insurance Group held cash and cash equivalents of $1.46 billion and total debt of $2.67 billion. During the year the company repurchased $78.4 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2026, management expects total revenue growth of 3% to 5%, driven by continued investment in Personal Lines and Commercial Lines.
