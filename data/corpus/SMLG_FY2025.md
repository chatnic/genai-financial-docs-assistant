## Business Overview

Summit Ledger Software (SMLG) is a core banking software provider headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Marcus Chen and Chief Financial Officer Olivia Lindqvist. As of the end of fiscal 2025, Summit Ledger Software had 2,233 employees and served approximately 2,308 thousand customers. The company reports results in 2 segments: Subscription, Professional Services.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2025, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Subscription**: revenue of $256.9 million, or 33% of the total.
- **Professional Services**: revenue of $519.3 million, or 67% of the total.

Professional Services remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2025.

**Third-party vendors.** We depend on third-party technology vendors, and an outage at a key provider could interrupt service.

**Competition.** Competition from fintech companies and large banks could pressure pricing and customer acquisition.

**Catastrophe losses.** Severe weather and natural catastrophes could produce claims losses well above our expectations.

**Regulation.** New regulations, including changes to capital and consumer protection rules, could increase compliance costs.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $776.2 million, which increased 14.5% from $677.9 million in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 30.0%. Net income was $177.1 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Professional Services segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2025, Summit Ledger Software held cash and cash equivalents of $389.8 million and total debt of $219.5 million. During the year the company repurchased $22.7 million of its common stock.

## Outlook

For fiscal 2026, management expects total revenue growth of 4% to 8%, driven by continued investment in Subscription and Professional Services.
