## Business Overview

Pinecrest Wealth Partners (PCWP) is a wealth and asset manager headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Marcus Abernathy and Chief Financial Officer Tomás Ramirez. As of the end of fiscal 2025, Pinecrest Wealth Partners had 4,182 employees and served approximately 2,968 thousand customers. The company reports results in 2 segments: Advisory, Asset Management.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2025, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Advisory**: revenue of $499.2 million, or 58% of the total.
- **Asset Management**: revenue of $355.8 million, or 42% of the total.

Advisory remained the largest segment by revenue.

## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2025.

**Credit quality.** Deterioration in consumer or commercial credit quality could increase charge-offs and require higher loss reserves.

**Talent.** Our growth depends on attracting and retaining engineering and risk management talent.

**Third-party vendors.** We depend on third-party technology vendors, and an outage at a key provider could interrupt service.

**Cybersecurity.** A cybersecurity incident could disrupt our systems, expose customer data and result in regulatory penalties.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $855.0 million, which decreased 1.1% from $864.7 million in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 23.2%. Net income was $150.9 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Advisory segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2025, Pinecrest Wealth Partners held cash and cash equivalents of $395.4 million and total debt of $667.9 million. During the year the company repurchased $10.2 million of its common stock.

## Outlook

For fiscal 2026, management expects total revenue growth of 6% to 10%, driven by continued investment in Advisory and Asset Management.
