## Business Overview

Atlas Consumer Lending (ATCL) is a consumer lender headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona. The company is led by Chief Executive Officer Hannah Chen and Chief Financial Officer Samuel Lindqvist. As of the end of fiscal 2024, Atlas Consumer Lending had 3,629 employees and served approximately 3,327 thousand customers. The company reports results in 3 segments: Personal Loans, Auto Finance, Credit Cards.

<!-- section: segment_results -->
## Segment Results

For fiscal 2024, segment revenue was as follows:

- **Personal Loans**: revenue of $329.2 million, or 26% of the total.
- **Auto Finance**: revenue of $495.3 million, or 39% of the total.
- **Credit Cards**: revenue of $459.1 million, or 36% of the total.

Auto Finance remained the largest segment by revenue.

<!-- section: risk_factors -->
## Risk Factors

The following are the principal risks management identified for fiscal 2024.

**Competition.** Competition from fintech companies and large banks could pressure pricing and customer acquisition.

**Third-party vendors.** We depend on third-party technology vendors, and an outage at a key provider could interrupt service.

**Credit quality.** Deterioration in consumer or commercial credit quality could increase charge-offs and require higher loss reserves.

**Talent.** Our growth depends on attracting and retaining engineering and risk management talent.

<!-- section: management_discussion -->
## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $1.28 billion, which increased 7.1% from $1.20 billion in fiscal 2023. Operating margin was 24.9%. Net income was $242.9 million. Management attributed the year's performance primarily to results in the Auto Finance segment.

<!-- section: liquidity -->
## Liquidity and Capital Resources

At the end of fiscal 2024, Atlas Consumer Lending held cash and cash equivalents of $325.5 million and total debt of $304.8 million. During the year the company repurchased $42.7 million of its common stock.

<!-- section: outlook -->
## Outlook

For fiscal 2025, management expects total revenue growth of 3% to 5%, driven by continued investment in Personal Loans and Auto Finance.
